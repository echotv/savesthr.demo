System Flow

The payroll-readiness flow

Each step unlocks the next — skip one, and Savest-Payroll has nothing to compute from.

1 · Company Setup Positions, Employment Status, and Salary Grades defined once.
2 · Employee Profile Required fields completed; Position and Status chosen from Company Setup.
3 · Movements Position, Status, and Salary Grade assigned — each a dated record.
4 · Weekly Schedule Work hours, breaks, and rest days — what payroll measures against.

Complete all four, and the employee's record is ready for Savest-Payroll to generate a pay run from.

SAVEST-HRIS03 / 10
Step 1 · Company Setup

Define the master data once

Positions, Employment Status, and Salary Grades are company-wide reference lists — every Employee Profile and every Movement reads from them.

PositionsEvery job title an employee can be assigned to, company-wide.
Employment StatusProbationary, Regular, and every other status an employee can hold.
Salary GradesGrade and step levels, with daily and hourly rates computed from basic salary.

These records must be complete before an employee can be recorded — Position and Employment Status are required fields on every Employee Profile, chosen from these exact lists.

Step 3 · Movements

Position, status, and pay —
each a dated record

After registration, Position and Employment Status change only through these screens — and Salary Grade exists only here. One open-ended record at a time; overlapping dates are rejected automatically.

Position Movement Promotions, transfers, and reassignments, each with its own date range.
HR Assistant Manager → HR Manager
Salary Grade Movement Grade and step changes, with daily and hourly rates computed automatically.
SG 1, Step 2 → SG 2, Step 1
Employment Status Movement Probationary → Permanent and beyond — fully dated, never overwritten.
Probationary → Permanent
